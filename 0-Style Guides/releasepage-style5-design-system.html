07 — Guardrails

Do / Don't

DoDon't
Keep the hero in full dark mode — deep ocean overlay, crisp white typeLighten the hero overlay or soften the dark-mode contrast
Use aqua consistently for every CTA on the pageIntroduce a second accent colour for buttons
Switch decisively to white/mist immediately after the heroBlend hero and intro backgrounds gradually
Use landscape, wide-format imagery throughoutUse portrait or square crops that break the coastal-wide feel
Keep typography geometric, sans-serif, confidentIntroduce any serif element anywhere in this style
