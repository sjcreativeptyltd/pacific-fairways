Style 1 — Bold & Confident

Volume / first-home-buyer market. Bold, direct, high-energy — built to convert fast. Maximum contrast, punchy type, solid colour blocks, no ambiguity. This system applies across all 9 sections of the fixed ReleasePage page structure.

07 — Guardrails

Do / Don't

DoDon't
Use full-bleed colour bands (Ink / Red / White) to divide sectionsUse whitespace alone as a section divider — no soft fades
Keep headings uppercase, tight, and 800-weight throughoutMix in sentence-case headings — breaks the billboard feel
Reserve red strictly for CTAs and the stat bandUse red as a decorative accent, icon fill, or link colour elsewhere
Keep buttons sharp-cornered (0px radius) everywhereRound any button or card corner — no border-radius anywhere in this style
Let geometry (lines, blocks, bands) carry visual interestAdd illustration, texture, gradients, or decorative icons
