07 — Guardrails

Do / Don't

DoDon't
Keep every heading sentence caseUppercase anything — breaks the conversational tone
Use generous padding and soft transitions between sectionsPunch with hard colour bands or sharp contrast
Use leaf/circle icons in green for bulletsUse sharp geometric markers or numbered lists where a soft icon fits better
Keep buttons rounded (8px) and lower-case-styledUse 0px radius or uppercase button text
Choose golden-hour, family, community imageryUse cold, clinical, or corporate-feeling photography
