Do / Don't

DoDon't
Open the hero with a coastal lifestyle image — beach, estuary, golden hourUse a standard estate aerial as the hero image
Include the Lifestyle Word Block as pure typography, no imagesAdd photos or icons to the word block — it should read as text alone
Include block frontage as a key stat alongside size and priceOmit frontage — it matters specifically to this buyer
Add the Brochure CTA Block as a secondary path before How to BuyRely on a single CTA path — this buyer often isn't ready to call yet
Keep every button and input fully rounded (30px)Mix in sharp or lightly-rounded elements — consistency of roundness matters here
