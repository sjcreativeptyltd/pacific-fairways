04 — Buttons

CTA styles

Primary — solid sage, parchment text
Secondary — earth outline
border-radius: 4px · text-transform: none (sentence case) · font-weight: 700 · padding: 15px 30px

Use hand-drawn-style connector lines in How to BuyUse sharp geometric numbered circles
