## STYLE VARIABLES — 5 PRESETS

Each preset covers a distinct market segment and tone. When a client doesn't provide branding or asks you to recommend a direction, pick the preset that matches their market and paste the BRANDING and DESIGN DIRECTION blocks directly into the main template.

All five are SEQ-market appropriate.

---

### STYLE 1 — BOLD & CONFIDENT
**Market:** Volume / first home buyer
**Tone:** Bold, direct, high-energy — built to convert fast
**Visual feel:** Strong contrast, punchy type, solid colour blocks, no ambiguity

```
BRANDING

Primary colour: #1A1A2E (Deep Ink Navy)
Accent colour: #E63946 (Signal Red)
Background: #FFFFFF
Secondary background: #F2F2F2
Body text: #1A1A2E
Font style: 'Array', sans-serif — Bold (700) headings, uppercase; 'Supreme', sans-serif — Medium (500) body. Self-hosted from public/fonts/ — do not load Google Fonts.
Button style: Solid red (#E63946), white text, sharp corners (0px radius), uppercase, wide letter-spacing
Logo: Development name as all-caps wordmark in primary colour

DESIGN DIRECTION

- Maximum contrast throughout — dark on light, light on dark, never grey-on-grey
- Headings are large, tight, and uppercase — they dominate each section
- Stat section: bold red background, white oversized numbers
- No decorative illustration or texture — geometry only (lines, blocks, colour bands)
- CTA buttons feel urgent — solid, sharp-edged, prominent on every section
- Section dividers use full-bleed colour bands rather than whitespace alone
- Hero overlay: dark navy (rgba 26,26,46,0.65) — text is crisp white
- This design should feel like it belongs on a billboard as much as a screen
```

## STYLE VARIABLES — 5 ADDITIONAL PRESETS (SET 2)

These five styles incorporate design principles observed from Calli Estate, Bloom Hervey Bay, Brookhaven, and The Rise. Each introduces at least one design pattern not present in Set 1.

New patterns introduced across Set 2:
- Sensory / immersive storytelling sections (Calli)
- Typographic word cycling / manifesto blocks (Bloom)
- Community proof and named sales team (Brookhaven)
- Park/green space as the primary product hero (The Rise)
- Dual logo lockup (developer + agent) in footer
- Panoramic/elevated view as visual centrepiece
- Developer quote/belief statement section

### STYLE 6 — PANORAMIC & ELEVATED
**Market:** Prestige hillside / view lots
**Tone:** Aspirational, view-obsessed, considered — the land itself is the hero
**Visual feel:** Wide horizontal layouts, full-bleed aerial photography, restrained type that steps back to let the view speak
**Key reference patterns:** Calli's panoramic framing, blank canvas positioning, sensory storytelling

```
BRANDING

Primary colour: #2C3E50 (Storm)
Accent colour: #D4956A (Terracotta)
Background: #FDFCFA (Warm White)
Secondary background: #F2EDE6 (Sandstone)
Body text: #2C2C2C
Font style: 'Gambetta', serif — Bold (700) headings; 'FamiljenGrotesk', sans-serif — Regular (400) body. Self-hosted from public/fonts/ — do not load Google Fonts.
Button style: Outlined terracotta (2px border #D4956A), terracotta text, 0px radius, wide letter-spacing, sentence case — fills to terracotta on hover with white text
Logo: Development name as elegant serif wordmark with thin horizontal rule below

DESIGN DIRECTION

- Hero is full-bleed panoramic: a wide aerial or elevated view image that bleeds to all edges — the land IS the hero
- Hero headline is minimal — 4–6 words max, large light serif, centred — do not compete with the image
- Hero overlay: very subtle warm gradient (bottom-to-top, rgba 20,15,10,0.40) — preserve sky detail
- Section 2 (Estate Intro): introduce a "blank canvas" framing — position the land as the starting point for the buyer's vision, not a finished product
- Add a SENSORY SECTION between Section 6 (Lifestyle) and Section 7 (How to Buy): 3–4 sensory vignettes (sight, sound, feel, space) — each with a small image, a one-word label, and 2 sentences of evocative copy. This is the emotional centrepiece of the page.
- Stat section: sandstone background, storm-coloured large numbers, terracotta stat labels — elegant not punchy
- Location section: lean into elevation, outlook, and views — "from your front door" framing
- Imagery direction: elevated aerials, golden hour valley views, wide sky, terraced landscaping
- Footer: dual logo lockup — developer logo left, agent/project marketer logo right, separated by a thin vertical rule
- This design should feel like a boutique residential project that someone would frame on their wall
```
