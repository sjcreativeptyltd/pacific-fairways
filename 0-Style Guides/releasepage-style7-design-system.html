07 — Guardrails

Do / Don't

DoDon't
Place the social proof stat bar directly below the hero, as its own bandOverlay it onto the hero image itself
Include a single authentic resident pull-quote after the stats sectionAdd a photo or multiple quotes — one quote alone carries the weight
Name the sales team simply — name, title, phoneOver-design the sales team block with bios or photos required
Use "Talk to our team" as the footer CTADefault to "Enquire Now" — it breaks the human, community-led tone
Keep every button and input pill-shaped (24px radius)Mix in sharp-cornered elements anywhere in this style
